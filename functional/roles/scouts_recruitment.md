# Scouts, talent evaluation and player personnel

## Job to be done
Identify players whose **observable football behaviors** warrant further scouting, even when traditional targets, receptions or rushing totals understate them.

## Highest-value questions / KPIs

| Question | KPI / evidence | Needed data | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does a player get open without being targeted? | Apparent untargeted opportunity proxy / pass routes | All eligible tracks + targets + QB timing | Highlights players to review |
| Does separation recur in comparable contexts? | Separation profile at defined moment + sample size | Opponent/defender tracking and context | Compare behavior, not just catch volume |
| Does a player create useful off-ball movement? | Observable defensive reaction and teammate space shift | Synchronized multi-player tracks | Finds possible uncredited contributions |
| Can their production be put in context? | Targets and receptions versus route/snaps counts | Participation + outcomes | Avoid raw-volume bias |

## Minimum functional behavior
- Shortlist, not automated draft grades; sort only by context-normalized and interpretable metrics.
- Always show **minimum sample count**, opportunity denominator, comparable group and at least one play.
- Note whether role (WR/TE/RB), QB, coverage, down/distance and opponent strength are controlled or unavailable.

## Decision supported
"Which player deserves additional film scouting and which specific plays demonstrate the behavior?"

## STOP / skip
If only targeted players exist or opportunities are not observable, drop untargeted-opportunity rankings. Do not treat apparent openness as guaranteed catch probability.

## Done when
A scout can compare two players with visible context and manually audit the underlying play IDs.
