# Reporters, sports journalists and media analysts

## Job to be done
Publish **a surprising, reproducible football story** that survives source-checking and skeptical questions.

## Highest-value questions / KPIs

| Story question | KPI / evidence | Data required | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| What does a box-score number hide? | Production vs contextual opportunity, transparently labeled | Targets/routes/tracking | Fresh angle beyond highlights |
| Is a play genuinely unusual? | Contextual ranking / percentile if comparable cohort exists | Adequate sample + cohort | Makes superlatives defensible |
| Was the effect repeated? | Repeated-play count / denominator | Comparable plays | Avoid anecdotal overreach |
| Can a reader verify the claim? | Metric definition, source play IDs, caveat | Provenance and join keys | Builds editorial trust |

## Minimum functional behavior
- Headline framed as a **finding with a number, reference group and sample**.
- Link/trace each claim to data definitions and example plays.
- Distinguish established NFL metrics from a genuinely distinct interpretation.
- Export a short text summary only if sources and uncertainty remain attached.

## Decision supported
"Is this insight accurate, interesting and safe to publish?"

## STOP / skip
No "record", "best ever", "caused" or "uncovered a flaw" claims without full comparable coverage and proof. Do not present synthetic values as measured NFL data.

## Done when
A skeptical editor can independently understand the numerator, denominator, timeframe and major limitation in under one minute.
